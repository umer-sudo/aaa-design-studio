    Monsoon specification · Karachi

    Where the rain gets in, and what stops it.

    Six places on this house where water enters after a Karachi downpour. Click a marker on the model, or a line here, for the build-up and the products.

    A layered specification, not a guarantee. Workmanship, curing, the flood test and each product's own datasheet decide whether it holds; the site engineer has the last word. Every figure and product is from a published source, listed on its sheet. Slopes and DPC rules are from an Indian practice guide, because no Pakistani code clause was found.

    Generated imagery is visualisation. Validated CAD/BIM geometry remains the source of truth.
